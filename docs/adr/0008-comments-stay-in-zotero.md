# Comments and notes are not shown on the tablet (for now)

Zotero highlight comments and note annotations stay in Zotero only. The tablet stores typed text as a single text flow per page rather than positionable boxes, typing on PDF pages is unconfirmed, and drawn-text renderings were judged too janky; "no comments rather than janky ones". Nothing is lost: comments remain on the Zotero annotations, which keep their identity across syncs.

## Future exploration

- A "comment note page" inserted after an annotated page, one typed paragraph per commented highlight, edited two-way.
- Tablet typed text mapped to Zotero `text` annotations.
