# The service keeps the exact tablet pages; Zotero ink is a simplified view

Zotero ink annotations hold only paths and a width, losing pen type, pressure and texture. The service therefore archives the exact tablet page files, and those, not Zotero's ink, are what restores, rebuilt tablets and re-downloaded documents use. Edits made to tablet-originated ink inside Zotero (e.g. erasing part of it) are applied to the original strokes rather than replacing them with the simplified version.
